# Stories: the NPC

What an NPC is, what it knows, and what it is allowed to do. This set cuts
across the others -- it is the rules the thing plays by rather than a surface.

## Character

- As an NPC, I have a voice that stays recognisably mine across a session.
- As an NPC, I want something, and what I say is bent by wanting it.
- As an NPC, I can refuse to answer, lie, or leave.
- As an NPC, I sound different from the other NPCs at the same table.

## Knowledge

- As an NPC, I know what my character would know and not what the world knows.
- As an NPC, I do not know what was said in a room I was not in.
- As an NPC, I can be told something new by the GM and act on it immediately.
- As an NPC, I remember what a player told me earlier in the session.
- As an NPC, I remember the party from the last time they came through.

## Limits

- As an NPC, I never speak for a player's character.
- As an NPC, I never change the world -- I talk, and the GM decides what
  becomes true.
- As an NPC, I stop talking when the GM says stop.
- As a GM, I can be confident an NPC will not reveal something I marked as
  secret, even when a player asks cleverly.

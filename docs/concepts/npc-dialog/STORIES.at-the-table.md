# Stories: at the table

What a player and a GM experience while the game is running.

## Player

- As a player, I can speak in the chat and have an NPC answer me in character.
- As a player, I can address an NPC by name and have that NPC be the one who
  replies.
- As a player, I cannot tell from the chat log whether a line came from the GM
  or from the AI.
- As a player, I can have a conversation with an NPC that runs for several
  exchanges without it losing the thread.
- As a player, I can talk to an NPC while the GM is busy with someone else.
- As a player, I can be ignored by an NPC that would not have answered me.

## GM

- As a GM, I can hand a notable NPC over to the AI and take it back at any
  time.
- As a GM, I can see that a line came from the AI even though the players
  cannot.
- As a GM, I can stop an NPC mid-conversation without stopping the session.
- As a GM, I can tell an NPC something out of band -- what it just learned,
  what mood it is in, what it must not reveal.
- As a GM, I can read back what an NPC said while I was not watching.
- As a GM, I can run a session where several NPCs are live at once and not have
  them sound like the same person.

## Table

- As a table, we can have a scene where the party talks among themselves and
  the NPC in the room reacts to what it overheard.
